# Code of Conduct

SYNVARO welcomes respectful, constructive participation.

- Be considerate and professional. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or publication of private information.
- Assume good intent where reasonable and help newcomers participate.
- Respect project maintainers' review decisions and GitHub's terms.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove abusive content and restrict participation to protect the community. To report conduct concerns privately, contact the maintainers through the GitHub organization contact method when available. Do not include sensitive personal information in public issues.

This is a lightweight interim policy; the project may adopt a standard community code as it grows.
