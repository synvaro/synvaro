# Product specification v0.1

**Status:** Draft for owner review. **Audience:** humans and AI development agents.

## Purpose
SYNVARO is an open-source, multi-brand content studio: plan → create → review → schedule → publish → measure.

## Pilot users
- International Safety Inspection Services Türkiye (ISIS) corporate brand and delicatessen shops: real product photography, promotions, recipes and reels.
- Qos Workspace: only verified released product behavior.
- Spexop-UI: actual OSS changes and releases.

## MVP user journey
1. Create organization and brand; configure brand tone and channel preferences.
2. Upload authentic media and verified product facts.
3. Draft content manually or with AI (user-provided API key).
4. Create channel-specific text/media variants and preview.
5. Approve the exact revision.
6. Schedule/publish to connected Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn or YouTube accounts, subject to platform approvals.
7. Record outcome, failures and external post IDs.

## Boundaries
In: multi-brand, content calendar, media library, AI-assisted creation, manual review, basic publishing.
Later: analytics, engagement inbox, integrations, automated campaigns, agency/multi-client SaaS billing.
Out: autonomous unreviewed publication, fictional product claims, paid ad management, universal support for every platform post type.

## OSS
Self-hosted Community should be useful end-to-end with bring-your-own AI keys. Commercial managed hosting and advanced automation are future options, not implemented entitlements.
